# Six levels of AI-native productivity

A framework from [Apoco](https://apoco.com) for recognizing how much AI has improved what a team, department or company gets done – and what to change next.

Productivity here means useful results for the full effort, time, attention and cost they take, review and rework included. The levels rank that benefit, not how advanced the tools are.

- **[Web page](https://apocohq.github.io/ai-native-productivity/)** – the six levels, a quick check and one team’s day at each level.
- **[Reader guide](framework/reader-guide.md)** – the levels with examples, a placement exercise and the evidence to look for.
- **[Levels in detail](framework/levels-in-detail.md)** – full conditions, company examples and evidence rules.
- **A4 handout** – [dark](handout/six-levels-a4.pdf) and [print](handout/six-levels-a4-print.pdf).
